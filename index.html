The summit selfie

  1. This is a summit selfie. Three friends, one mountain, one photo.

  2. To your phone, it's just two million coloured dots. So why would anyone print it and frame it?

  3. Rewind. 5:40 am. The alarm goes off. Nobody wants to get up.

  4. Kilometre 4. Rain. Someone wants to turn back.

  5. Kilometre 7. A blister. Everyone keeps going anyway.

  6. The top.

  7. That's what the photo is worth. Not the pixels: the hike. The photo is a receipt for something that really happened.

  8. Now imagine AI made the exact same photo. Same faces. Same mountain. Same pixels.

  9. But nobody climbed anything. It's a receipt for nothing. Worth: zero.

Utility Assets

Other things are valuable simply because they're useful.

  1. ↑ Head north for 400 m
  2. ↱ Turn right onto Station Road
  3. ↻ At the roundabout, take the 2nd exit
  4. 📍 Destination is on your right

Who wrote these directions? A person? An app? AI?

You don't care. You care that they get you there.

2,847+ 1,3964,243

Same with a correct sum. Calculator, human or AI: the answer is just as good.

A Utility Asset is judged on three things:

  • ✓ Is it correct?
  • ✓ Is it clear?
  • ✓ Is it useful?

Who made it doesn't change its value. But someone still has to check it's right, and that someone is you.

So what about school?

Your teacher doesn't need another essay on the causes of the First World War.

They've read hundreds. What they need is evidence that the thinking happened in your head. That's what homework is for.

Almost everything you hand in is a Process Artifact. Your essay is your summit selfie.

Using AI on a Process Artifact is like bringing a forklift to the gym. The weights go up. You don't get any stronger.

Where AI can help you

When you need a Utility Asset for yourself: questions to quiz yourself on, a revision timetable, a unit conversion, a different explanation of something you're stuck on. The useful thing comes from AI. The learning still happens in you.

Each task's instructions come first. If you're not sure, ask your teacher.

Teachers too

The same rule applies to your teachers.

AI can help make these

  • 📝 A vocab quiz
  • ➗ A worksheet of practice questions
  • 🖼️ A slide template
  • ✉️ A trip-logistics letter home
  • 🪑 A seating plan

They're Utility Assets. You need them to be correct and clear, not handmade.

These must come from your teacher

  • 💬 Personal feedback on your work
  • 📋 Your report comments
  • 🎓 A university reference

They're Process Artifacts. Their whole value is that a real person read your work and thought about you.

Feedback is your teacher's summit selfie.
